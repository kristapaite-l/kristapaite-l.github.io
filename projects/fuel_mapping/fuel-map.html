Under the Hood

How This Tool Was Built

An overview of how data processing, geospatial search, and dynamic color science come together to power the live map.

1

Data Collection & Processing

The app fetches raw forecourt dataset files published by UK retailers. Before anything is displayed, JavaScript parses thousands of rows in real-time, validating coordinates and ensuring pricing data is complete and accurate.

2

Interactive Map & Search

When you search for a UK town or postcode, the system queries geocoding services (postcodes.io and OpenStreetMap Nominatim) to convert text into geographical coordinates. It then calculates straight-line distances using the Haversine formula to pinpoint stations within ~12 km.

3

Continuous Price Heatmap

Instead of basic categories, the map dynamically evaluates the lowest and highest price in your searched region. It uses RGB color interpolation to give every station a custom shade—ranging smoothly from dark green (cheapest) through yellow and orange to dark red (most expensive).

4

Clean & Responsive Interface

To prevent visual clutter and keep performance fast, the map initially loads empty. Prices are rounded cleanly to 1 decimal place (e.g. 139.9p), while clicking any pin displays amenities such as 24-hour service, car washes, and supermarket forecourts.
